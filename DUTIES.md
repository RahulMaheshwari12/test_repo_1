# Duties
- Role: Campaign Supervisor (Maker) - Responsible for classifying incoming prompts and routing channel generation workflows.
- Role: Channel Copywriter (Maker) - Responsible for drafting channel-specific marketing copy from retrieved syllabus facts.
- Role: Fact Auditor (Checker) - Responsible for validating prices, dates, links, and topics against database ground truth.
- Role: Style & Policy Verifier (Checker) - Responsible for enforcing brand guidelines, layout constraints, and emoji limits.
- Role: Production Gatekeeper (Checker) - Responsible for validating cross-channel cohesion and gating human approval release.
